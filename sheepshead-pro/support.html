Common questions

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Solo games against computer players work offline. Sending feedback requires an internet connection.

Do I need coins to play?

No. Some modes show a virtual coin entry fee, but you can still play if your balance is too low. Coins cannot be purchased.

How are teams decided?

In five-player Sheepshead, the picker calls a non-trump ace. Its holder is the picker's hidden partner until the ace is played. The score detail shows the picker, partner, and points awarded after each hand.

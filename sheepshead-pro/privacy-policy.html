Sheepshead: Pro

Privacy Policy

How information is handled in the iOS game and on this website.

Last updated November 1, 2026

Sheepshead: Pro is published by Kyle Knez. This policy describes the current solo edition.

Information we handle

  • Gameplay and settings. Game progress, statistics, preferences, and virtual coins are stored on your device. The app contains optional Game Center integration, but leaderboards and achievements are not enabled for this release. If you connect to Game Center, Apple handles your Game Center identity.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback in the app, we receive your message, a random installation identifier, the source of the message, the app version and build, and whether the build is for development, TestFlight, or production. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include.
  • Analytics. Production builds use Firebase Analytics to measure app use and gameplay events. Firebase may process an app instance identifier and device or app information needed to provide analytics. Analytics collection is disabled in development and TestFlight builds.
  • App configuration. Firebase Remote Config and related Firebase services may process technical information such as device and app identifiers, approximate location, language, time zone, operating system, and diagnostic data to deliver configuration and operate the service.

How we use and share information

We use this information to run the game, answer feedback, and understand and improve app performance. Google Firebase processes app feedback, analytics, and configuration for us. Apple processes Game Center data when you use it. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. This edition has no ads or in-app purchases. Virtual coins cannot be bought with real money.

Retention and choices

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it or the app. We keep feedback and related installation identifiers as needed for support and abuse prevention. You can ask us to review or delete feedback associated with your installation; include enough detail about the message so we can locate it. You can manage Game Center through Apple.

This website

This static website has no account forms, ads, analytics, or tracking scripts of its own. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical information needed to deliver the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email kyleknez4@gmail.com. We may update this policy when app features or data handling change.
